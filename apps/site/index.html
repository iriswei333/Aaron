Our story

Built by a parent, for the 7am question.

SproutCue started at a kitchen table in Seattle, with a toddler asking "what are we doing today?" and a parent scrolling through weather apps, event calendars, library pages and group chats for an answer.

We built one place that knows your kids, your neighborhood and the forecast — and gives you a plan in seconds. Then we added the parts we loved most: the families down the street, and stories that make our kids feel like heroes.

FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

Who is SproutCue for?

Parents and caregivers of babies, toddlers and young kids who want quick, local ideas for the day — and a few more friends at the playground.

Where does SproutCue work?

We're starting in the Seattle area, where family events and library story times are built in. Playgrounds and playdates work around whatever neighborhood you set.

How do playdates stay safe?

Playdates are tied to real family accounts, and you choose who can see the playdates you host. Always meet in public places like parks and libraries.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Photos you add to Play Studio are stored privately for your family and used to create your books and stories. You can delete them at any time.

Can I add more than one child?

Yes. Add each child to your family profile so plans, stories and age ranges fit everyone.

Is there a mobile app?

SproutCue works in any browser today, and native iPhone and Android apps are on the way.
