# SproutCue Parent Authorization and Optional Consent (Draft)

**Draft for legal and product review — not a substitute for an implemented consent flow.** This service is intended for adults only. A parent or caregiver enters and controls the child-related information; children may not create accounts or use the service directly.

## A. Direct notice to parent

**Subject:** Please review SproutCue’s use of information about your child

Hello **[parent name]**,

You are receiving this notice because you created or requested an adult SproutCue account using **[email address or phone number]**. SproutCue is a parent-only planning service. Children under 13 may not create accounts, submit information, use chat, create play dates, or participate directly.

We would like to collect the following information about **[child nickname/name]**: nickname or name; birthday or age; home city; food preferences and allergies; diaper size; feeding stage; daycare days; favorite activities; caption language and tone; and the parent’s choice about whether the child’s real name may be used in a caption. If you use optional features, we may also process a location or city, play-date details, messages, and photos or videos.

We would use this information to provide the family planner, personalize meals and activities, find nearby places and events, manage private or public play dates, enable parent-to-parent chat, save family plans, and draft captions locally from your selected preferences. We do not use children’s information for behavioral advertising or sell it.

The information may be stored and processed by **[list each operator and contact information]** for hosting, authentication, security, maps/places, and weather/events/resources. Other authenticated users may see information included in a public play date, and intended chat participants may see messages or media. You may consent to internal personalization without consenting to optional public sharing or parent-to-parent chat. **[Confirm whether any disclosure is integral and describe it precisely.]**

Please read the [SproutCue Privacy Policy](**[privacy-policy URL]**). It explains the information collected, purposes, disclosures, retention/deletion schedule, security practices, and your rights to review, delete, or stop further collection or use.

If SproutCue is required to obtain verifiable parental consent for a particular practice, we will not activate that practice until the verification process below is complete. If a consent request is not completed within **[reasonable period]**, we will delete the contact information collected only for that request. You may withdraw authorization or any optional consent at any time by contacting **[privacy email/phone]**.

## C. Verification method — implementation requirement

SproutCue must use one of the following methods, chosen and documented by the operator, before activating collection of children’s personal information:

- signed consent form returned by fax, mail, or electronic scan;
- payment method that provides separate transaction notice to the account holder;
- toll-free call staffed by trained personnel;
- video conference with trained personnel;
- government-ID check against a database, with the ID deleted after verification;
- knowledge-based challenge questions that are difficult for someone other than the parent; or
- verified photo-ID method meeting the applicable requirements, with verification photos deleted after matching.

If SproutCue uses an email or text reply method, it should be limited to a flow where the child’s information is used only for internal purposes and is not disclosed to third parties, followed by a confirmation through the same method or by letter/phone. Because SproutCue includes optional third-party processing, public play dates, and chat, email-only consent should not be used as the sole method for those disclosures without legal review.

## D. Consent record to retain

SproutCue should retain a consent record—not unnecessary identity documents—with the notice version, policy version, exact choices, timestamp, parent contact, child profile identifier, verification method and result, disclosures approved, and any withdrawal or deletion request. Update the record and send a new direct notice before materially changing an approved collection, use, or disclosure practice.
