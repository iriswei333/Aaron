## 2. Adult-only service and children’s privacy

SproutCue is an adult-only planning service designed for parents and caregivers of young children. Only an adult may create, access, and manage an account. Children may not create accounts, submit information, use chat, create play dates, or otherwise participate directly in SproutCue. We use reasonable measures to communicate and enforce this restriction, including **[describe adult age screen, account terms, and any verification]**.

Parents may enter information about their children. Under COPPA, the analysis generally turns on whether personal information is collected online from a child; information collected directly from a parent is treated differently. We nevertheless apply child-privacy safeguards to information about children under 13 and will obtain parental authorization before optional disclosures or other practices where applicable law requires it. We will not knowingly collect information directly from a child under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate parental consent, contact us at **[privacy email]**. We will investigate and, where required, delete the information.

## 3. Information we collect

We collect only information needed for the purposes described below.

### Information provided by a parent or caregiver

- Account information, such as email address, display name, authentication records, and sign-in or verification information.
- Child-profile information entered by the parent, such as a child’s nickname or name, birthday or age, home city, diaper size, daycare days, favorite activities, caption language and tone, and the parent’s choice about using the child’s real name in captions.
- Saved external weekend-event decisions, such as attendance status, event title, venue, date/time labels, and the source URL.
- Location information, such as a city, address or place entered by the user, and precise latitude/longitude when the user chooses “Use current location.”
- Play-date information, such as playground, address, coordinates, date and time, visibility, age range, capacity, notes, host, and participants.
- Parent-to-parent messages and media shared in chat. Media selected for chat may be encoded in the message and stored by the service.
- Social-planning information, such as links to albums or social accounts, selected media file name/type, caption preferences, and locally drafted captions. Media selected for local caption drafting is previewed in the browser and is not uploaded for that feature.
- Requests sent to us, including parent access, correction, deletion, or consent-withdrawal requests.

### Information collected automatically or from the device

- Authentication cookies and security/session information.
- Browser-local values, including the login email and selected Home background, stored in local storage.
- Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as request metadata, approximate network information, and error logs. **[Confirm whether production logging or analytics tools collect IP addresses, device identifiers, cookies, or similar identifiers, and list each tool here.]**

SproutCue does not intentionally collect a child’s voice, biometric information, advertising identifier, or behavioral profile. Do not upload or enter those data unless the parent has reviewed and approved the applicable use.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- authenticate parent accounts and provide the SproutCue service;
- personalize play planning, resources, and locally drafted captions for the selected child;
- save and display attended weekend events and user-created content;
- find nearby playgrounds, weather, and family events when the user requests those features;
- create, display, and manage public or private play dates;
- enable parent-to-parent chat for people connected through a play date;
- provide local caption drafting based on the parent’s selected preferences;
- maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service;
- comply with law and respond to valid legal requests; and
- communicate with a parent about the account, consent, security, and service changes.

We do not use children’s personal information for behavioral advertising, sell it, or use it to make the child’s information publicly searchable. We do not use a child’s information for a purpose unrelated to the purpose for which the parent consented without first providing notice and obtaining any consent required by law.

## 6. Public and shared features

Public play dates can reveal the playground, address, coordinates, date/time, age range, notes, host label, and participant count to authenticated users who can view that public play date. Private play dates are limited to the host and joined participants under the app’s access controls.

Parent-to-parent chat can include text, photos, and videos. Chat content is available to the sender and recipient and may remain in the service until deleted under the retention schedule below or until a parent requests deletion. Do not share a child’s full name, home address, school, medical record, or other sensitive information in a public play date or chat.

## 7. Parental consent and direct notice

Before collecting, using, or disclosing personal information from a child under 13 when COPPA applies, we will send the parent a direct notice describing the information, purposes, disclosures, privacy policy, consent method, and deletion of the parent’s contact information if consent is not completed within **[reasonable period, e.g., 14 days]**. We will obtain verifiable parental consent using a method reasonably designed to confirm the parent’s identity.

Consent to internal collection and use is separate from consent to disclosures to other users or third parties, unless the disclosure is integral to the feature. A parent may refuse optional public sharing, chat, and other optional disclosures while still allowing the child profile to be used for internal personalization where legally permitted.

- stop a child’s information from being collected or used going forward.
